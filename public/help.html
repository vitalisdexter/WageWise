How to use WageWise

Set up your pay, log overtime by work date and check what you were paid. Use the Planner to see what upcoming paydays could look like.

Set up your pay

  1. Choose Create account, pick a username and set a password of at least 12 characters. If you already have WageWise records, move them into your account during setup or from Settings.
  2. Go to Settings → Basic pay and enter your weekly basic pay. Use Active deductions for amounts taken after tax and National Insurance.
  3. In Weekly overtime cutoff, enter the last work date covered by a known payslip and the date that payslip was paid. Set your Default OT1 rate and save.

The cutoff date itself is included. Work from the day after the cutoff goes into the next weekly pay period. Confirmed shift selections from a payslip check take priority.

Log the overtime you worked

  1. In Calendar, tap the date you worked, then Log overtime.
  2. Enter hours and minutes, or enter start and end times with any unpaid break. An overnight shift is recorded against its start date.
  3. Check the hourly rate and tap Save shift. You can add several shifts to one date. Each saved shift keeps its own rate.

What the calendar markers mean

OT
You saved overtime for that work date.
Pay
You have a saved pay entry dated that day. A date can show both Pay and OT.
Off
You marked the date as a day off.
0
You marked the date as worked without overtime.
!
A workday in your schedule still needs a log entry.

Estimate your take-home pay

  1. In Calculator, choose the date you expect to be paid. Use the logged overtime for that payday or add hours manually to try a scenario. If hours are already included from your log, leave them out of the manual entries to avoid counting them twice.
  2. Under Tax & National Insurance, check your Scottish suffix-code allowance. For example, S1257L uses £12,570.
  3. Review gross pay, PAYE, National Insurance and net pay under Your earnings. Save this week’s pay adds an estimate to Dashboard → Earnings history.

Which year-to-date figures to enter

Use Payslip → Latest payslip totals to enter the pay date, taxable pay to date and PAYE paid to date exactly as printed on your latest payslip. WageWise uses those totals for eligible upcoming calculations.

When using the calculator’s YTD override to reproduce a payslip, use totals from before the payment. Subtract that period’s taxable pay and PAYE from its printed YTD amounts, then enter the preceding tax week. Prior NI is optional.

The calculator estimates weekly Scottish PAYE and category A National Insurance for 2025/26 and 2026/27. It shows a Week 1 estimate when it lacks complete prior totals. You can still record actual payslips with other pay schedules or tax codes.

Import and check a payslip

  1. Open Payslip → Import payslips and choose a PDF, photo or screenshot. A PDF with selectable text or a clear, straight image makes the figures easier to read.
  2. Use View original to check the extracted pay date, pay frequency, printed tax week or period, gross and take-home pay. Correct any missing or misread fields.
  3. Use Add payslip line for missing items. Keep the printed description and choose a category that matches how the employer treated it. For example, choose Back pay, Bonus, Other deduction for a service charge, or PAYE refund for returned income tax. Use Reimbursement after tax for expenses added to take-home pay.
  4. Choose Save actual payslip. If WageWise finds an entry for that payday, review it and choose whether to replace it or keep the new one as a separate supplementary payment.

Saved figures appear in Dashboard → Earnings history and on the calendar pay date. You can enter figures yourself with Record actual pay. To print a WageWise summary, choose Current calculation or Saved entry and tap Print.

Check the overtime against your log

Choose Check against my log, select the payslip’s pay date, and review the suggested shifts. Use Pick from other logged dates for exceptions. Enter the overtime hours and amount on the payslip, then tap Save comparison to see any difference.

Plan your next paydays

Forecast

Compare up to four upcoming weekly paydays with basic pay only, logged overtime, and logged plus planned overtime. Enter planned hours as a total for each work date. As you record hours in Calendar, WageWise reduces the remaining plan for that date.

Net-pay goal

Choose a payday and enter your take-home target and the extra overtime you can work. WageWise checks 15-minute increments and shows the smallest addition that reaches the target or the estimated shortfall. Select Add to plan to assign the extra time to eligible dates before confirming.

Payday pots

Choose a forecast or enter your actual take-home pay. Add weekly and monthly bills, then split the remainder across named pots. Monthly bills are converted to a weekly reserve using the monthly amount × 12 ÷ 52. Pot percentages can add up to 100%, with any unused share shown separately.

Under Investment scenarios, enter your contribution, time horizon, annual return and fee assumptions. Compare contributions alone, your chosen return and a downside case.

Install WageWise and get reminders

  1. On Android, open WageWise in Chrome and use Settings → Install app → Add to Home Screen when available. On iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari.
  2. Go to Settings → Workdays and reminders. Select your working days and reminder time, tick Remind me to log overtime, allow notifications and save.
  3. Tap Send a test notification to check delivery. A daily reminder opens the relevant work date. Saving overtime, marking No overtime or marking Day off completes that day.

The installed web app picks up updates when opened online. Sign in online to access your records.

Move records, back up or recover access

Move older WageWise records

If you used a pairing code, choose Use this paired device from account setup on that device, or enter the previous code. Review the record counts before moving the workspace. For records kept only on the original browser, start a new workspace, then open Settings → Your account → Move my local records on that browser.

Sync and back up your work

Sign in with the same username on your other devices. Check the sync status under Settings → Your account. Original payslip files stay on the device where you imported them. To download or restore records, use Settings → Backups and review the preview before confirming a restore.

Reset your password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter your username. Contact the administrator privately to verify your request. After receiving the single-use code, choose I have a reset code and enter it within 30 minutes to set a new password.
